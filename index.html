Online Headbanger Detection System

A static browser demo using MediaPipe Face Landmarker. It layers pose, geometry, expression, distance, movement, frequency, and behavioural summary measures on top of the live face overlay — all processed locally in your browser.

All processing happens locally in your browser: camera frames never leave your device. On first load the page fetches its fonts and the MediaPipe runtime and models from public CDNs (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and Google's model store). Nothing is recorded unless you press Record, and recordings stay on your device as a CSV download.
